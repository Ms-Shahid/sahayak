# ClassOS Product Overview & Blueprint (Simplified 2-Tier School Focus)

## 1. Product Vision & Positioning

ClassOS is a syllabus-grounded learning platform built exclusively for K-12 schools and school districts. It eliminates administrative software bloat to focus 100% on core learning operations, curriculum alignment, and trusted AI-assisted instruction.

## 2. Simplified 2-Tier Syllabus Architecture

Syllabus creation and content management are organized into a clean, 2-tier cascading structure:

- **Tier 1: School / Department Level (Master Baseline)**
  - **Managed By**: Department Heads, Subject Lead Teachers, or Curriculum Directors.
  - **Purpose**: Establishes school-wide academic standards, approved exam board specifications, master textbook PDFs, and official formula/reference guides.
  - **Role**: Serves as the overarching baseline ensuring consistency across all grade levels and sections.
- **Tier 2: Class / Grade Level (Active Teaching & Learning)**
  - **Managed By**: Grade and Subject Teachers (e.g., all teachers handling Grade 10 Mathematics).
  - **Purpose**: The single operational space for active teaching, content ingestion, practice generation, and tutoring for an entire grade level.
  - **Content Ingestion**: Multi-format uploads including Documents (`.pdf`, `.docx`, `.txt`), Presentation Decks (`.pptx`, `.pdf`), and Images/Scans (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.heic` of whiteboard notes, handwritten solution steps, and textbook diagrams).
  - **Role**: Eliminates section-level fragmentation and duplication. Upload once to Grade 10 Math, and all students in that grade access the same unified, high-quality content.

## 3. Core Non-AI Learning Primitives

- **2-Tier Content Repository**: Structured `Subject` -> `Unit` -> `Chapter` -> `Topic` hierarchy across School and Class levels.
- **SIS/LMS Roster Sync**: Automated mapping of students to Class/Grade learning batches via ClassLink, OneRoster, Google Classroom, and Canvas.
- **Practice & Homework Hub**: Assignment distribution, multi-format student submissions (handwritten photo uploads, PDFs), and a split-screen grading/markup desk.
- **Topic Mastery Matrix**: Real-time tracking of student topic understanding against the syllabus at the Class and Department levels.

## 4. AI-Grounded Learning Architecture

- **Teacher AI Content Studio**: Converts uploaded notes/slides/scans into visual slide decks, audio/video summaries, infographics, and practice quizzes/flashcards.
- **Interactive Teacher Approval Workflow**: Side-by-side review studio displaying proposed AI practice questions alongside exact source proof citations from uploaded materials. Allows instant approval, quick edits, or steering prompts before publishing to students.
- **Student AI Tutor**: Zero-hallucination Q&A tutor grounded strictly in Class (Tier 2) and School (Tier 1) materials. Features step-by-step hint mode and method-aligned problem solving.
- **Teacher Assistant Agent**: Compiles daily end-of-class recap summaries for students and data-backed debriefs for teachers with targeted next-day warm-up suggestions.
