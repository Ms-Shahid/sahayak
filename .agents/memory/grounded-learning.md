---
name: Grounded learning limits
description: Safety boundary for AI learning outputs and citation claims
---

Never equate a valid source quotation with a guaranteed correct AI answer, and do not promise zero hallucinations.

**Why:** The PRD asks for strictly grounded learning. Exact quote matching verifies source provenance, not whether every generated inference is correct.

**How to apply:** Preserve teacher approval for published practice, reject unverified quotations, show unavailable-source states, and describe AI output as assistance requiring review.
