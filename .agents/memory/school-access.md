---
name: School data ownership
description: Why legacy demo records must not be assigned to newly enrolled schools
---

Never infer ownership of pre-authentication demo records from a school name or a former demo role.

**Why:** The original ClassOS MVP had no verified users or owners. Automatically attaching those records to a newly signed-in administrator would create an unverified data claim.

**How to apply:** Leave legacy synthetic records quarantined. Any future import or ownership recovery needs an explicit, verified process rather than matching a school display name.
