---
name: Administrator handover boundaries
description: Why handover requires an existing teacher and deliberately clears the outgoing administrator's class access.
---

Administrator handover is a transfer of school responsibility, not an invitation
to enroll an outside identity or a general role editor. Keep the recipient an
existing teacher in the same school, and give the former administrator no
implicit class access.

**Why:** Being the original enrolling account establishes school administration,
not a verified teaching assignment. Automatically retaining all classes after
demotion would preserve much of the authority the transfer was meant to remove.
An external replacement would also need a separate, verified school-membership
decision rather than using handover to bypass enrollment boundaries.

**How to apply:** Preserve these conservative boundaries when extending handover.
The successor can explicitly assign classes to or remove the former
administrator. Broader role editing or outside-account succession requires its
own authorization design.
